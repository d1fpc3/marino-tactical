Class Descriptions

From your first concealed carry permit to advanced defensive tactics, here is every course we offer, what it covers, and what to bring.

Flagship Course

Delaware Concealed Carry / Permit to Purchase | $125

This full-day course prepares students for Delaware's CCDW permit and Handgun Qualified Purchaser requirements. The certificate satisfies State Bureau of Identification training mandates.

Classroom Topics

  • Firearm safety, handling, and secure storage with child-safety emphasis
  • Safe shooting fundamentals and live-fire protocols
  • Skill development and maintenance techniques
  • Federal and Delaware firearms laws, including deadly force
  • Criminal avoidance and conflict de-escalation
  • Suicide prevention considerations

Range Work

Live-fire exercises with a minimum of 100 rounds.

Additional Instruction

Application filing tips, handgun nomenclature, concealment methods, dry-fire drills, reciprocity resources, and tactical mindset development.

Required Equipment

  • Handgun (rentals available)
  • 2 magazines or speed loader
  • Holster (loaners available)
  • 100 rounds of ammunition
  • Eye / ear protection (loaners available)
  • Range-appropriate clothing (no suits, skirts, sandals, heels)
  • Sturdy belt and cover garment

Out-of-State Permits & Advanced Training

Utah Concealed Firearms Permit | $75

Half-day course for those 21 and older. Recognized by the Utah Bureau of Criminal Identification. Includes fingerprinting and a passport-style photo. Just $50 as an add-on to the Delaware class.

Maryland Wear and Carry | $125 per class

Full-day courses addressing Maryland's 16-hour requirement (two classes; our DE CCDW covers the first 8 hours). Covers safety, state and federal laws, deadly force, application tips, concealment, and tactical mindset. Includes the Maryland HQL at no additional charge.

NJ Concealed Carry Permit | $75

Half-day course. Classroom instruction covers safety, laws, and conflict resolution. Range qualification required with your intended carry firearm or firearms.

Advanced CCDW Tactics | $225

Full-day, limited to 10 students, for valid CCDW permit holders. Covers close-quarters tactics, reloading, malfunction clearing, weapon retention, and movement scenarios. Requires 200 rounds, a concealed carry holster, and a cap or brimmed hat.

NRA Basic Courses

Basic Handgun, Shotgun, and Rifle courses, each issuing NRA certificates and covering nomenclature, fundamentals, and safe handling principles.

Discounts Available

We offer discounts for military veterans, first responders, teachers, and groups of 5 or more. Call to ask about your rate.
