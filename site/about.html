About Marino Tactical Training

A Delaware-based company built on real-world experience, professional standards, and a commitment to the law-abiding citizen.

Our Mission

Professional, Realistic Training

We are a Delaware-based company specializing in firearms training, defensive tactics, and concealed carry courses. Our mission is to provide professional, realistic training to the law-abiding citizenry of Delaware and surrounding states.

Every course combines hands-on live-fire exercises with expert legal instruction, so you leave not only able to shoot accurately, but confident in the laws that govern carrying and using a firearm in self-defense.

Your Instructor

Our classes are taught by Kirk Marino, an active law enforcement officer and military veteran with nearly 18 years of experience teaching concealed carry and firearms instruction. All of our instructors are active law enforcement officers and military veterans.

That background means you are learning from people who carry professionally every day, understand the law from the inside, and know what actually matters when it counts.
