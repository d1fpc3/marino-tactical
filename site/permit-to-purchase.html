Delaware Handgun Permit-to-Purchase Training

Certified instruction for the Delaware Handgun Purchaser Permit. The same class also satisfies the CCDW training requirement, so you can cover both in a single day.

Marino Tactical Training provides the certified instruction needed for obtaining a Delaware Handgun Purchaser Permit. The program also addresses CCDW permit requirements, allowing participants to satisfy both in a single class.

What This Course Covers

  • Firearm safety and handling
  • Storage and child access prevention
  • State and federal firearm laws
  • Deadly force and self-defense law
  • Conflict avoidance
  • Live-fire range qualification

Common Questions

Does this course satisfy the Permit-to-Purchase requirement?

Yes. Completing this course provides the certified training required for the Delaware Handgun Purchaser Permit.

Does it also cover CCDW?

Yes. This same class satisfies the Delaware CCDW training requirement.

Who Should Take This Course

Delaware residents seeking to purchase handguns or obtain CCDW permits, including beginners. Certain groups are exempt, including active and retired law enforcement, FFL holders, armored car guards, licensed security personnel, correctional officers, active military, NRA instructors, hunter-safety certified individuals, and competitive shooters with appropriate credentials.

Format: Full-day classroom instruction plus live-fire range exercises in Felton, Delaware. Certificates are issued upon completion.
