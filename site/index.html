How It Works

From Booking To Carrying In Three Steps

No guesswork. We handle the training and walk you through the paperwork.

01

Book Your Date

Call or message to reserve a seat. Class sizes are small and capped, so dates fill quickly. Bring your own firearm or rent one from us.

02

Train Live-Fire

Spend the day in the classroom on law and safety, then qualify on a real range with a 100-round live-fire course taught by an active LEO and veteran.

03

Leave certified, with hands-on help completing your permit application so you can carry with confidence.
