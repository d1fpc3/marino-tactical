Who We Are

Trained By Operators

We are a Delaware-based company specializing in firearms training, defensive tactics, and concealed carry courses. Our classes are taught by Kirk Marino, an active law enforcement officer and military veteran with nearly 18 years of experience teaching concealed carry and firearms instruction.

Our mission is simple: provide professional, realistic training to the law-abiding citizenry of Delaware and surrounding states, delivered with hands-on live-fire exercises and expert legal instruction.
