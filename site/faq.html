Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on permits, costs, reciprocity, and eligibility. Still have a question? Call 302-542-3755.

Delaware concealed carry permits, or CCDW permits, are offered to Delaware residents only. Review the eligibility requirements, download your application, and complete a required course of instruction. We provide this training and review the CCDW process during class.
Check your state's issuing authority website. Utah has the greatest reciprocity program and allows a permit holder to carry in approximately 36 states.
Costs include the application fee ($65 plus notary), newspaper public notice ($20 to $119), background check and fingerprinting (about $70), course fee ($125 to $250), passport photos ($5 to $15), and ammunition and supplies ($20 to $50).
Our class is $75. That includes all the required materials, fingerprinting, and a photograph. If taken as an add-on to the Delaware class, it is only $50. Application fees are not included.
The Delaware CCDW course is valid for Delaware, plus Virginia, Florida, Arizona, and other states' non-resident permits. The Utah CFP permit is similarly valid across multiple jurisdictions.
Reciprocity is when one state honors the CCDW permit of another state. Carriers must research and follow the applicable laws in the jurisdictions they visit.
Utah has reciprocity with approximately 36 states, though some only honor Utah resident permits, not non-resident permits.

Minor violations typically do not affect approval. More serious violations, such as reckless or aggressive driving convictions, DUIs, or repeated dangerous moving violations such as speeding, may result in permit denial.
