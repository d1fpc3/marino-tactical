Delaware CCDW Permit Training

Certified training for Delaware residents and non-residents seeking a Concealed Carry Deadly Weapon permit and legal carry within Delaware.

Marino Tactical Training offers certified instruction for Delaware residents seeking a Concealed Carry Deadly Weapon (CCDW) permit, serving both residents and non-residents interested in legal carry within Delaware.

Why Train With Us

  • Exceeds the Delaware CCDW training requirement
  • Professional instruction covering firearm safety and applicable laws
  • Live-fire exercises on a shooting range
  • Application assistance for permit processing
  • All instructors are active law enforcement or military veterans

Eligibility for Delaware CCDW

  • Delaware residents aged 18 and older, demonstrating good moral character
  • Non-residents holding reciprocity permits may legally carry in Delaware
Already hold a valid Delaware CCDW permit? You bypass the Permit-to-Purchase requirement entirely.

Course At A Glance

  • Format: Full-day classroom + live-fire range
  • Location: Felton, Delaware
  • Course fee: $125
  • Equipment: Handgun, magazines, ammunition, holster, protective gear, cover clothing
  • Rentals: Handguns available
